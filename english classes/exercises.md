## 1. Present Perfect (have/has + particípio)

### 1.1 Complete com have/has + o verbo na forma correta

1. She ______ (finish) the report already.
2. I ______ (never / see) this error in the system before.
3. We ______ (already / send) the PO to the supplier.
4. He ______ (not / reply) to my email yet.
5. ______ you ever ______ (work) with SAP before?
6. They ______ (not / face) this issue in production yet.
7. I ______ (know) Julia for five years now.

### 1.2 Encontre e corrija o erro (erros reais seus)

1. "I did some tests in QA environment and didn't faced with this issue."
2. "Did you ever seen this kind of bug before?"
3. "She has went to the meeting already."
4. "I have finish the task."
5. "Have you did this configuration before?"

## 2. Concordância sujeito-verbo (foco em "you" e sujeitos plurais)

### 2.1 Escolha a forma correta

1. You (is / are) responsible for this PO.
2. These materials (was / were) included manually.
3. He (don't / doesn't) usually work on weekends.
4. The reports (shows / show) the same error.
5. You (has / have) to check the system first.
6. You (was / were) right about the root cause.
7. The two orders (need / needs) approval.

### 2.2 Corrija a frase

1. "You is right about the issue."
2. "These two materials was included in the PO by you manually."
3. "He don't have access to the system."
4. "You was late for the call yesterday."
5. "This issues happens with every PO."

## 3. Verbos irregulares (decoreba - sem padrão fixo)

### 3.1 Passado simples

1. know → ______
2. weep → ______
3. freeze → ______
4. sting → ______
5. swim → ______
6. spin → ______

### 3.2 Complete a frase com o verbo no passado

1. Yesterday the system ______ (freeze) twice during the test.
2. She ______ (know) the answer immediately.
3. He ______ (swim) across the lake last summer.
4. The wasp ______ (sting) him on the arm.
5. The wheel ______ (spin) out of control.
6. She ______ (weep) when she heard the news.

---

## 4. Falsos cognatos / vocabulário

### 4.1 Escolha a palavra certa

1. The tests were run in the QA ______ (ambient / environment).
2. He was ______ (upset / sad only) when the shipment didn't arrive — pode significar chateado OU derrubar algo, dependendo do contexto.
3. She was ______ (humming / hammering) a song while working, not using a tool.

### 4.2 Traduza a frase, evitando o falso cognato

1. "Eu rodei alguns testes no ambiente de homologação." → I ran some tests in the ______.
2. "Ele ficou chateado com o atraso." → He was ______ about the delay.
3. "Ela estava cantarolando enquanto trabalhava." → She was ______ while working.
